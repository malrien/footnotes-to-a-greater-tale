{/* Malrien calls him "Master Maurevelle". */}

An Elezen of Sharlayan was Master Maurevelle, learned in ancient tongues and in the manuscripts of eras long past. So many years did he spend upon the old texts that he came to speak as they do, and even his colleagues deemed him antiquated. The field he preferred to the library.

He came into the midst of my people in search of a mystery: a people who had, of a sudden, set aside the names of their lineage. At first, he was exactly that which we feared: a stranger who wrote down all he saw. His place he earned through patience. Never did he ask for a true name, and his notes contained only the names we give unto the world.

An oath of brotherhood he swore with one of my kin, and thus received that one's face and true name, and with them the duty of guarding that family as his own blood. No other outsider in living memory hath been granted as much. A mask he never wore, for he was not of the blood; a kinsman without a face, we called him.

When Sharlayan withdrew its colony from Eorzea, he refused the order to return. Stubbornness, his colleagues deemed it. 'Twas not so: to leave would have been to abandon the family he had sworn to protect. Never did he explain himself, for to explain would have meant speaking of true names.

<Note tilt={3} position="center" width="70%">
  Patient to the point of vexation, and pedantic about words. Gentle with the curious, merciless with the idle. He would correct one's grammar in the midst of a grave conversation, and quote authors centuries dead as though they were old friends.
</Note>

His manuscripts on my people he kept in a box wherein no true name was ever written.

When last I came home, he was gone, and all his things with him.
