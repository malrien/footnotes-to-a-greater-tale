Worn by my people before all strangers. Once they were all alike, so that none might tell kin from kin; now each is singular, carved and painted with the signs of its wearer's station and deeds, and one of us can read a mask as one readeth a story. A mask is mended and added to throughout a life, never replaced. A child receiveth the first when first they meet the outer world, together with their outside name.
