term: "Archon"
kind: term
draft: false
---

A title of Sharlayan, conferred upon those who complete their studies at the Studium; its bearers carry marks upon the body. To be enrolled as one, my true name and lineage did I give unto an archive that any scholar may consult. The elders of my people liken it unto the ledgers of the Era of Records, and going home hath not been simple since.
