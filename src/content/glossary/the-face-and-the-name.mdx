---
term: "The Face and the Name"
kind: term
draft: false
---

The first law of my people. One's face and true name are one's family, and are shown only to those who are kin or become so, by marriage, adoption or an oath of brotherhood. Face and name are always given together, and whoever receiveth them taketh on the duty of guarding that person and theirs as their own blood. At a funeral all unmask, that the dead may depart knowing the faces of those who remain.
